Plan detail / Advertising

Ads with
clear limits.

Pavio plans use different ad levels to distinguish the subscription experience. Core and Pro are ad-free.

PlanAd levelIncluded formats in plan notes
BasicHeavyBanner, sidebar, sponsored cards, video, corner
StandardLightBanner, sidebar, sponsored cards, less frequent video, corner
CoreNoneNo ads
ProNoneNo ads
Always visible

Clearly labeled

Advertisements should be identified as ads and remain visually distinct from school and browser content.

Sound

No auto audio

Video advertisements should not start with sound without the user choosing to play them.

Schoolwork

Don’t interrupt

Ad placements should not obstruct assignments or interfere with schoolwork or submission flows.

The original plan notes describe consistent ads that rotate periodically, rather than constantly changing placements. The examples above describe the planned format and rules.
