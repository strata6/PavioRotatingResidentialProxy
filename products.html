04 / Network

PRRP

Pavio Residential Proxy Relay has usage allowances on Standard, Core, and Pro. Allowances are capped separately by week and month as listed on Pricing.

See PRRP details ↗

Residential hosting is on by default

PRRP usage allowances describe how much relay usage a plan includes. They do not grant permission to use a customer’s residential connection. Residential hosting is enabled by default across all plans. Only Pro includes a setting to turn it off. Hosting is separate from using PRRP for your own browsing; usage allowances are listed by plan.
