Quick answers

Good to know.

Common questions about Pavio plans, ads, and residential proxy participation.

What does each plan cost?

Basic is $2 per month or $10 per year; Standard $4 or $20; Core $6 or $30; and Pro $8 or $40. Annual prices are yearly totals.

See pricing ↗
Which plans include PRRP usage?

Standard includes 2 GB per week and 5 GB per month; Core includes 5 GB per week and 15 GB per month; Pro includes 10 GB per week and 30 GB per month. Basic has no PRRP use allowance.

Read the PRRP guide ↗
Is there a daily PRRP cap?

No daily usage limit is listed. Weekly and monthly limits still apply independently.

How does residential hosting work by plan?

Hosting is enabled by default on Basic, Standard, Core, and Pro. Pro is the only tier that includes a setting to turn hosting off.

Review hosting defaults ↗
What is the difference between PRRP use and hosting?

PRRP use is the subscriber's capped access to relay services. Hosting is a separate choice to contribute a residential connection. Having a usage allowance does not mean hosting is active or required.

Which plans include Camo and Games?

Standard, Core, and Pro list Camo and Games as included products. Basic does not list them as included.

Explore products ↗
Which plan is ad-free?

Core and Pro are ad-free. Standard has light ads, and Basic has heavy ads.

See ad details ↗
Where can I see the education access list?

The plan notes list Canvas, NoRedInk, and GMetrix for Basic; add IXL and Blooket for Standard; and add i-Ready for Core. Pro is described as having access to all websites.
