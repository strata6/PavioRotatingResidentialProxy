PRRP: usage and hosting

Using PRRP and hosting through PRRP are separate. Basic has no PRRP usage access. Standard includes 2 GB weekly / 5 GB monthly; Core 5 GB / 15 GB; Pro 10 GB / 30 GB. Residential hosting is enabled by default on all tiers. Only Pro includes a setting to turn hosting off. Review the hosting behavior and the separate usage limits before choosing.
