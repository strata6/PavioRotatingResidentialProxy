What makes it Pavio

Made for
student life.

A student-first companion for reaching learning sites, organizing study time, and practicing at your own pace.

01 / BROWSER

A focused study session

Move between coursework, lessons, and practice from one focused starting point. Your plan sets which supported sites are included.

02 / CAMO

Keep it yours

Camo adds a more discreet visual mode to your Pavio experience, for moments when you want a quieter screen.

03 / GAMES

Play between tabs

A lighter side of the browser. Pavio Games brings quick entertainment into your browsing routine.

04 / PRRP

PRRP use & hosting

Eligible plans include a capped amount of PRRP usage. PRRP use has plan-specific caps. Residential hosting is enabled by default on all tiers; only Pro can turn it off.

05 / FLEXIBILITY

Pick your level

Choose from Basic, Standard, Core, and Pro, with clearly stated monthly or annual pricing.

06 / TRANSPARENCY

Know the details

See ad levels and weekly and monthly usage limits before choosing a plan. Review the hosting default and usage limits before choosing.
